# Threat Model & Security Analysis (`docs/THREAT_MODEL.md`)

## 1. Scope & Assets Protected

1. **Confidential Compensation & Payroll Records (`payslips`)**: Monthly salary breakdowns, tax deductions, and masked bank details.
2. **Personal & Residential Data (`employee_profiles`, `addresses`)**: Residential/mailing addresses, masked national IDs, masked tax PANs, and employment hierarchy.
3. **Authentication & Session Credentials (`users.password_hash`, `sessions.token_hash`, `sessions.csrf_token`)**: Credentials and session state governing role access.
4. **Leave Entitlements & Workflow Integrity (`leave_balances`, `leave_requests`)**: Prevents unauthorized leave approval or balance manipulation.
5. **Audit Trail Integrity (`audit_events`)**: Immutable accountability records with `correlation_id` and actor tracking.

## 3. Encryption & Data Protection Assumptions
- **In Transit**: In production (`NODE_ENV=production`), TLS termination is assumed at the reverse proxy/load balancer, and the backend automatically enables `Strict-Transport-Security` (HSTS) and the `Secure` + `__Host-` prefix flags on the session cookie.
- **At Rest**: Supabase PostgreSQL encrypts database volumes and backups at rest (AES-256). Passwords are stored exclusively as salted adaptive bcrypt hashes (`cost = 12`), and session tokens are stored exclusively as SHA-256 digests. Sensitive national IDs, tax PANs, phone numbers, and bank account numbers are stored and displayed in masked format (`XXXX-XXXX-4105`).
